ℹ️ About Otechy

Otechy Ltd

Innovative Malawian technology — digitalising Malawi through open-source and free digital resources.

Otechy is an innovative Malawian technology enterprise founded in 2025 by a visionary group of student entrepreneurs during their post-MSCE academic holiday. Spurred by a collective mission to digitalize Malawi through the strategic leveraging of open-source and free digital resources, the team conceptualized and executed a high-utility software ecosystem.

Beyond engineering platforms like ScoraHub and the KUHeS Yaza Hub, Otechy operates as a beacon of youth empowerment — a living proof of concept to young Malawians nationwide that structural focus and technical dedication can yield world-class technological solutions with zero initial capital.
